# Trinetra Safety Disclaimer and Guidelines

## ⚠️ Critical Safety Notice

**Trinetra is an experimental assistive awareness prototype, NOT a certified medical device, protective equipment, or mobility aid.**

1. **Never Replace Traditional Mobility Tools:**
   - Trinetra MUST NEVER be used as a replacement for a white cane, guide dog, orientation and mobility (O&M) training, or human sighted guides.
   - Do not rely on Trinetra for navigation in unfamiliar, hazardous, busy, or traffic-dense environments.

2. **No Guarantee of Detection or Obstacle Clearance:**
   - Cameras have inherent limitations including blind spots, lighting sensitivity (glare, deep shadows, darkness), field-of-view boundaries, and potential latency or frame dropouts.
   - The system CANNOT guarantee that all obstacles, elevation changes (drop-offs, curbs, stairs, holes), moving vehicles, or hazards will be detected or communicated in time.

3. **Software & Hardware Reliability:**
   - Computer software, operating systems, and USB devices are subject to unexpected crashes, driver stalls, operating system updates, battery depletion, and disconnects.
   - Users must always remain aware of their physical surroundings through tactile, auditory, and cognitive senses.

4. **Testing Environments:**
   - All testing during early development sprints must take place in controlled, safe indoor spaces with zero traffic or fall hazards, accompanied by a sighted partner or safety observer.
