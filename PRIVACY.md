# Privacy Policy for Trinetra (त्रिनेत्र)

Last updated: November 2026

Trinetra is committed to the highest standards of privacy and data autonomy for individuals with visual impairments and disabilities.

### 1. 100% Local Processing (Zero Data Collection)
- **No Cloud Transmission**: All camera feeds, microphone inputs, sensor data, and neural inference occur strictly on the user's local hardware device (127.0.0.1).
- **No Telemetry or Tracking**: Trinetra does not log, track, store, or transmit personal identities, browsing history, GPS locations, or voice recordings to any external third-party server or cloud provider.

### 2. Camera and Sensor Inputs
Camera frames are processed in-memory for real-time hazard detection, sign language translation, and banknote recognition. No video frames are persistently saved unless the user explicitly triggers the local Incident Recorder for personal emergency logging.

### 3. Open Source Transparency
All source code for the Trinetra platform is public under the MIT License at https://github.com/taksyastudio-gif/trinetra. Anyone can inspect and verify our local-first privacy guarantees.
